# Transformation Pattern Template

![Transformation Pattern](https://img.shields.io/badge/Transformation-Pattern-blue)
![Technology Strategy](https://img.shields.io/badge/Technology-Transformation-green)
![Operating System](https://img.shields.io/badge/Transformation-Operating%20System-purple)

This template is used to document recurring technology transformation patterns observed across organizations.

These patterns form part of the **Transformation Operating System**, a broader framework for connecting strategy, governance, transformation, execution, and delivery.

---

## Pattern Name

Short descriptive name for the transformation pattern.

---

## Context

Describe the organizational or technical situation where this pattern appears.

Examples may include:

- legacy systems reaching operational limits  
- organizations adopting cloud platforms  
- fragmented platforms creating operational inefficiencies  
- introduction of AI capabilities  

---

## Problem

Describe the challenge organizations encounter in this context.

This section should clearly articulate the core transformation problem.

---

## Forces

Describe the constraints shaping the situation.

Examples include:

- operational continuity requirements  
- regulatory constraints  
- technical debt  
- limited engineering capacity  
- organizational resistance to change  

---

## Solution Pattern

Describe the structured approach organizations use to navigate the transformation.

Provide practical steps or stages involved in the transformation.

---

## Expected Results

Describe the outcomes typically achieved when the pattern is applied successfully.

Examples:

- improved system scalability  
- reduced operational complexity  
- stronger organizational alignment  
- increased innovation capacity  

---

## Relationship to the Transformation Operating System

This pattern supports the **Transformation layer** of the Transformation Operating System:

Strategy  
→ Governance  
→ **Transformation Patterns**  
→ Execution  
→ Delivery
